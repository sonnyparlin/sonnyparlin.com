It never got easier. I got better at being uncomfortable.

I walked onto the mat for the first time on September 4, 2007, in West Hartford, Connecticut. I was 34. I had no idea that a single class would turn into the thing that organises the rest of my life. Royce Gracie tied my blue belt on in 2009. On July 30, 2026, Professor Robby Donofrio tied on the second stripe.
