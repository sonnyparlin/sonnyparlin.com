It never got easier.

I walked onto the mat for the first time on September 4, 2007, in West Hartford, Connecticut. I was 34. I had no idea that a single class would turn into the thing that organizes the rest of my life.

Royce Gracie tied my blue belt on in 2009. I moved to Florida, found Rob Kahn, and spent the next decade getting promoted by him, one hard-won belt at a time. On July 30, 2026, Professor Robby Donofrio promoted me to second degree.

“I just got better at being uncomfortable. That is the whole lesson.”Sonny Parlin
